FS HW Monitor {VERSION}
{UNDERLINE}

A live temperature dashboard for your PC: CPU, GPU, RAM, SSD and fans, with
gauges and high / average / low for each. Made for the little screen inside a
PC case, but it works on any monitor.


INSTALL

1. Run FS-HW-Monitor-Setup-{VERSION}.exe

   Windows may show "Windows protected your PC", because the app isn't
   code-signed yet. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

2. Leave "Install the PawnIO sensor driver" ticked. CPU, RAM and fan
   temperatures need it. (It isn't shown if you already have it.)

3. Click Install. The dashboard starts when setup finishes.


USING IT

- It finds a small in-case screen (1280x800, 1920x480, 800x1280 and others)
  on its own and fills it. Without one, it opens as a normal window.

- Right-click anywhere for the menu. "Settings..." has everything: theme,
  layout, which cards to show, warning temperatures, fan names, night mode.

- Hover "Most used" on a card to see which apps use that part the most.

- Double-click a card to reset its high / average / low.

- Want closing to hide it instead of quitting? Turn on
  Settings > General > "Closing hides to the tray". Click the tray icon by
  the clock to bring it back.

- Make your own theme: right-click > Theme > Open themes folder, copy
  OceanTemplate.json, rename the copy and edit it. Every setting is explained
  inside the file.

- It asks for administrator rights each time it starts, because that's
  needed to read the sensors. To skip the prompt, turn on
  Settings > General > "Start with Windows".


UNINSTALL

Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > FS HW Monitor > Uninstall.
